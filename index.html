How it starts

Automatic: when driving starts you get a chime and two taps. 1) Is a child in the car? "Yes" means the app checks when you park, "No" keeps it quiet, and no answer counts as unknown, so it still checks when you park. 2) Is this drive routine or exceptional (a different driver, time or place than usual)? Exceptional turns on extra care: it always checks when you park, escalates twice as fast, and isn't used to teach the app your routine. Turning "every drive" off asks only at your usual times, which is quieter but gives less protection on unusual days. Manual: the app doesn't ask at the start. Use the "Child is in the car" switch in Settings.

Sounds

Stages: 1) soft chime when it asks. 2) After 60 s, a spoken reminder with the child's name, or your own voice. 3) After 2 min, an urgent alarm. 4) After 3 min, a message to your contact. Heat (25°C+) makes everything twice as fast.

Child tag (optional)

If the tag stays in the car while your phone walks away, the alarm starts fast. Off by default.
How it works: the tag has no on/off switch to forget. If it was in range during your drive, the app stays quiet when you park and watches for 10 minutes. If the tag is lost for 20 seconds in that time (you walked away without the child), it goes straight to the spoken reminder, the urgent alarm after 20 seconds, and the family alert after 1 minute. If you carry the child with you, the tag stays near and nothing happens. Without a tag, the app uses the routine question as before. To test: turn it on, tap "tag in range", "Start driving", "Driving → walking", then "tag lost". Real pairing works only in Chrome on Android, with this page open. Background scanning needs the native app.

    How it works: each contact installs the free ntfy app (Android or iPhone), taps +, and subscribes to the secret name above. When the alert fires, all four phones ring together (urgent priority) with a map link to the car. Anyone with the secret name can read the channel, so share it only with these four people. "Text everyone now" opens your messages app with all four numbers filled in, since a web page can't send texts by itself.

    Roles and handover

    Each child has one person on duty. Everyone else is backup. Only the person on duty is asked and alarmed; backups get the family alert.
    Demo: switch who you are to play both sides of a handover on one phone. On real phones each person is simply themselves. Add your name in Settings and your contacts first.
    The second box is the child's usual carer.
    A handover counts when the other person accepts, and until then you stay on duty. "I have (child) now" is for when you already have the child. Add an end time and, when it passes, the app asks the carer to hand the child back; nothing switches by itself. If someone other than the usual carer is on duty, their drives are marked exceptional automatically. With roles in use, only the person on duty is asked at the start and at parking.

    Zones are an estimate from the outside temperature, not a measurement of the car. Green: 5 to 15°C, normal timing.
